# 15 — User guide (customer)

**For:** Evermore customers.
**Date:** 2026-08-13. Written against what the system actually does today; a
feature that is not built yet says so.

---

## 1. Creating an account

1. Register with your email, a password of at least 12 characters, your name
   and your phone number.
2. We email you a confirmation link. **You must confirm your email before your
   first order** — you can browse the menu without it.
3. Your phone number is stored in international form (`0812…` becomes
   `+62812…`) so the courier can always reach you.

## 2. Adding a delivery address

This is the step that decides whether we can serve you, so we do it before you
try to order rather than after.

- Every address needs a **pin on the map**. The pin is what we route by; the
  written address is what the driver reads. An address without a pin cannot be
  saved.
- The moment you save it, we tell you **which kitchen will serve you** and how
  far away it is — or that we do not deliver to that area yet.
- You can save as many addresses as you like: home, office, a parent's house.
  Lunch to the office and dinner to home is a normal pattern and each delivery
  carries its own address.
- Add a note for the driver ("grey gate, ring the bell") — it prints on the
  manifest.

## 3. Ordering meals

1. Open the menu for the diet type you want. Each day shows one **meal**: a
   main dish plus whatever sides, dessert or drink are scheduled with it.
2. The kcal and protein figures are the **total for the whole meal**, added up
   from every dish in it. If one dish has no nutrition panel yet, the meal is
   marked as an estimate rather than pretending to be exact.
3. Choose your quantity, your address and your delivery time.
4. **One order can cover several days.** The price tier is worked out on the
   total number of meals in the order, so Monday-to-Friday × 2 meals is priced
   as ten meals, not as five orders of two.
5. The price you see includes tax.

### The cut-off

Orders for a given day close at **18:00 the day before**. The menu shows a live
countdown. The cut-off is checked on our server, so changing your device's
clock does not extend it.

## 4. Paying

Payment is by bank transfer.

**Transfer the exact amount shown, including the last three digits.** Those
three digits are your unique code — they are how we match your transfer to your
order. If you round the amount, we cannot match it automatically and your order
waits.

1. Transfer to the account shown on your order page.
2. Upload your transfer proof (JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 5 MB).
3. We confirm it, usually the same day. You will receive an email.
4. If we cannot confirm it, we tell you why and you can upload again.

**Unpaid orders expire.** You have two hours, or until the cut-off if that comes
sooner — the page tells you which. When an order expires, the slot goes back to
other customers.

## 5. Packages

A package is a number of prepaid meal credits.

- **One credit buys one meal**, whatever that meal contains. A meal with four
  dishes costs the same one credit as a meal with one.
- Your credits become usable **when we confirm your transfer**, not when you
  order — so a weekend of waiting does not eat into your validity.
- The validity period starts on that same day.
- Book meals whenever you like while credits remain and the package is valid.
  Each booking uses one credit.
- You can **skip a delivery before the cut-off** and the credit comes back.
  After the cut-off the food is already being prepared, so it does not.

### Please note

**Unused credits are not refunded and do not roll over.** When a package
expires, any remaining credits are lost. We warn you by email a few days
beforehand. We do not refund money for any order — if something goes wrong, we
put it right in credits.

## 6. Getting help

Your order page shows the WhatsApp number for customer service. Quote your
order code (for example `FQWGE927`) and we can find it immediately.

## 7. Not built yet

Being straight about what does not exist today:

- WhatsApp notifications (email only for now)
- The calorie/TDEE calculator
- Allergen warnings against your saved profile
- Rating a meal after delivery
- Your weekly nutrition chart
- Referral and voucher codes
